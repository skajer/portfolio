About me

I specialize in industrial automation (IIoT), production process digitization, and modern AI system implementation.

I bridge industrial hardware with modern software:

  • Industrial IoT & Node-RED: Extensive experience with existing and new Node-RED flows, integrating measurement devices, sensors, and industrial protocols (Modbus RTU/TCP, Serial/COM, MQTT, TCP/IP).
  • Visualization & Analytics: I build stable statistical process control (SPC) systems and Grafana dashboards for real-time measurement data on production floors.
  • Agentic AI & MCP: I use n8n, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and LLM agents to automate complex business and analytical workflows.

Whether you need an audit and fix of existing Node-RED infrastructure, integration of micrometers and pneumatic sensors, or a custom AI agent built from scratch — I deliver reliable, scalable, battle-tested solutions.
